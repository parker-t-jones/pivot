# Deep-link candidates

Watch links open with `Linking.openURL` (`app/contexts/SwitchingContext.tsx`). Nothing here is wired into the app. A custom scheme is used only after `Linking.canOpenURL` succeeds, then the https link is the fallback. `canOpenURL` for a custom scheme returns false unless that scheme is listed in `LSApplicationQueriesSchemes`. Adding one is an Info.plist change and **requires a new native build**; an OTA update cannot see it.

"Parker tested on device" stays blank until a phone confirms the link opens the installed app on the right screen.

Sources are the page or post that actually states the URL. Community posts are not vendor docs.
